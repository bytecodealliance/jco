Portfolio parse and warm calls improve materially in both bundlers, while
link/evaluation regresses; initialization is inconclusive under the declared
5% and noise rules. Async/future warm-call effects remain inconclusive, including
the future interval just below one: its effect is too small. A portfolio warm
iteration calls all eight workloads, so its cost is an aggregate, not a single
component or payload-byte cost. Paired ratios need not equal ratios of absolute
medians. See [all expanded absolute metrics](n13-expanded-metrics.json), including
the separate cold native ESM bundle-import/initialization diagnostic. It excludes
process launch, core compilation and import/shim setup; it is not browser or
end-to-end latency evidence. These results are specific to this Node/CPU/toolchain.
